// how your set is built

Built for one Arrangement, not scene-per-song

Most bands running a click or backing tracks alongside real instruments keep the whole set in one Arrangement, a Locator per song, not Session view's scene-per-song setup. It's not simpler for its own sake: a locator is one unambiguous point on a linear timeline, there's no scene design or follow actions to get right, and it plays back exactly the same way every night. If that's how your set's built, Markerlist reads it directly, no reconfiguring needed.

// why it holds up on the night that matters

A wedding doesn't get a second take.

No command sent while you're mid-song puts the show at risk — the session stays exactly as you built it, no matter what happens to the network.

Never modifies your project

Markerlist reads and controls your session in real time. Nothing is ever written back to your .als file — the arrangement you rehearsed is the one that plays.

Recovers from a dropped WiFi

The venue's network isn't yours to fix mid-song. If the connection drops, the device reconnects and picks up exactly where the show is — nothing fires late.

A panic button, built in

Two quick taps on STOP bring the whole show back to the first song instantly, whatever state it's in — the recovery move for when something goes sideways.

The rest of what a show needs

Reorder without touching Ableton

Drag songs into a new order between sets, or on the spot. Pull one out and it moves to the library — bring it back with one tap if the night calls for it.

Section loops

Hold a chorus or a bridge with one tap when the room's into it — once, twice, or freely until you release it, with a clean hand-off back to the song.

Team pre-roll

Coming back from a break, the whole band sees the song name, then a tempo-timed countdown — everyone comes in together, not just whoever's near the screen.

Share the setlist with the client

Export the set as a PDF with your band's name and total duration — hand it to the couple, the planner, or the venue ahead of the night.

Questions bands actually ask

What if the venue's WiFi is unreliable?

The device reconnects automatically and picks up the exact state of the show in progress. No command sent during the drop ever fires late once the network is back.

Can I swap songs mid-set if the crowd wants something else?

Yes — drag any song up next, or pull one from the library, without pausing the show or touching Ableton.

My set is in Session view with one scene per song — does this still work?

Not directly. Markerlist reads Locators in a single Arrangement, not Session scenes. If you're open to switching, most bands running a click or backing tracks find it's actually the simpler setup for a fixed, night-after-night set.

Do we need Max for Live or any other plugin?

No. Markerlist connects directly to Ableton Live — nothing to load into your session.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes — the Discovery tier is free: 15-minute sessions with Standard Multi features unlocked, separated by a 5-minute pause, up to 5 restarts per 24h, so you can test it under real conditions before choosing a paid tier.
