// frequently asked questions

Before you get started

Does Markerlist modify my Ableton project?

No, never. Markerlist reads and controls your session in real time but never writes anything to your original project — you can uninstall the app without leaving any trace in your .als file.

Do I need Max for Live or a special plugin?

No. Markerlist connects directly to Ableton Live, with no plugin or device to load into your session.

Does Markerlist slow down when Ableton is under heavy load (high CPU)?

No. Unlike a Max for Live device, which runs inside Ableton's audio engine and can be affected by its load, Markerlist controls your session from the outside through a dedicated channel (Remote Script MIDI/OSC) — the STOP command, for example, was measured 4x more stable over MIDI than over OSC.

What happens if WiFi drops during the show?

The device reconnects automatically and immediately picks up the exact state of the show in progress. No command sent during the drop ever fires late once the network is back.

How many devices can I connect at the same time?

It depends on your tier — from 1 device (Access) to 4 (Standard Multi), with an Unlimited add-on available on Standard Multi.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes — the Discovery tier is free: 15-minute sessions, separated by a 5-minute pause, up to 5 restarts per 24h, so you can test the app under real conditions before choosing a paid tier.
