Nothing about your session changes, ever

Whoever's running sound on Sunday can reorder the set, jump songs, or recover from a dropped connection — none of it touches your original Ableton project.

Never modifies your project

Markerlist reads and controls your session in real time. Nothing is ever written back to your .als file, so there's no risk of Sunday's set getting altered by accident.

If the connection drops mid-song, the device reconnects and picks up exactly where the show is. No command ever fires late once the network is back.

Two quick taps on STOP bring the whole show back to the first song instantly, whatever state it's in — the safety net for when something goes sideways.

Ready ahead, flexible live

// built for the actual service

The rest of what a Sunday needs

Team pre-roll

Before coming back in, the whole team sees the song name, then a countdown timed to the tempo — everyone comes in together, not just whoever's watching the screen.

Reorder without touching Ableton

Drag songs into a new order for this week's service in seconds. Pull one out for next time — it moves to the library and can be brought back with one tap.

Section loops

Loop a chorus or a bridge with one tap when the pastor keeps the moment going a little longer — instant and infinite, released the moment they wrap it up. Need an exact repeat count instead? Set it right on the section.

Section cue

Feeling led to skip a verse or repeat the bridge? Arm a cue on any section, tap where you want to go next, and MarkerList reroutes playback the moment you leave it — no scrambling in Ableton mid-service.

Printable run of service

Export the set as a PDF with your team's name and total duration — hand it to whoever's filling in this week without opening a laptop.

// before you get started

Questions worship teams actually ask

What happens if the sanctuary WiFi drops mid-song?

The device reconnects automatically and picks up the exact state of the service in progress. No command sent during the drop ever fires late once the network is back.

Can we let musicians see the setlist without giving them control?

Yes — that's what device roles are for. The sound desk keeps play/stop, everyone else gets a read-only view of the current and next song.

What if this week's volunteer has never used it before?

There's nothing to configure on the app itself — the setlist is already built from your session's locators. Export a PDF run-of-service ahead of time if you want them to see the order before they touch a screen.

Do we need Max for Live or any other plugin?

No. Markerlist connects directly to Ableton Live — nothing to load into your session.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes — the Discovery tier is free: 15-minute sessions with Standard Multi features unlocked, separated by a 5-minute pause, up to 5 restarts per 24h, so you can test it under real conditions before choosing a paid tier.
