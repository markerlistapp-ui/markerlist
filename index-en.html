Why Markerlist

Built for the stage, designed for fluidity

01

Instant Customization

Change the order of your tracks before or during your set with a simple drag-and-drop, without ever interrupting playback.

02

PC/Mac Host & Touch Control

Your computer runs the Ableton session in the background, while you control everything remotely from your tablets or phones with seamless fluidity.

03

Standalone, No Max for Live

MarkerList works entirely independently. No Max for Live installation required: just a lightweight and stable integration for your setup.

Key Features

Drag & Reorder

Modify your setlist on the fly

Drag, drop, and reorganize your markers in a snap, even while playback is running in your session.

Library & Management

Adjust your show live

Remove unwanted tracks from your set: they instantly move to the library and can be re-integrated with a simple click.

Playback & Automation

Stop management and continuous playback

The app automatically stops playback at the next marker without complex MIDI programming, or chain your songs for seamless, uninterrupted playback.

Live View

Maximum visual comfort on stage

Ideal under spotlights: see at a glance the current track, the next one, the automatic countdown based on the click, and the remaining time.

Multiplayer & Sync

Connected band spirit

Every musician gets real-time access from their own tablet or phone to vital concert information and Ableton tracks (mute, volume, routing).
