Spoken titles and sections for your set.

Drop your Ableton Live set. This page lists the song and section names MarkerList can announce, speaks the ones the app does not already have, and gives you a ZIP to drop into the app's voice folder.

Your set, your names and the voice stay in this tab: none of it is uploaded.

Step 1

Your names

Song titles are the locators (END_LIVE excluded). Sections are the clips of the track named SECTION. This page reads the saved file: save your set in Live first (Cmd/Ctrl+S).

    Step 4

    Download

    Unzip it and put the files in the app's tts_custom folder: ~/Library/Application Support/MarkerList/tts_custom on macOS, %APPDATA%\MarkerList\tts_custom on Windows. MarkerList finds a clip by its file name, so keep the names as they are.

    Made for MarkerList.

    MarkerList runs your Ableton Live setlist from the stage: choose the song, loop a section, start and stop from a phone, a tablet or a MIDI pedal. It announces the next section out loud, and the clips you make here are the ones it plays for your own section names.
