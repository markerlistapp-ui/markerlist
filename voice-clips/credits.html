What stays on your computer

Your Live set, the names in it and the texts you type are read and spoken inside this browser tab. They are never uploaded. The only large download is the voice model (about 92 MB), fetched once and then kept by your browser. This tool puts no restriction of its own on how you use the clips you create.

This page counts visits like the rest of the website, and only if you accept cookies. Your set and your texts are never part of that. Privacy policy.

eSpeak NG and its source

The complete corresponding source is available from this site: the unmodified source of the 1.52.0 tag (commit 4870adfa25b1a32b4361592f1be8a40337c58d6c) as espeak-ng-1.52.0-source.tar.gz (also at github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng), our two patches (one, two), the build script and the exact versions of the tools in BUILD-INFO.txt (see also the explanation).
